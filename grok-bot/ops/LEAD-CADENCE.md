## Syncopation (the beats)

The grid is uneven on purpose. Do not flatten it into one timer.

| Beat | Feel | Do |

| **Board** | Call and response | Critic Strict comments. Forge implements. Dialog until PASS or HOLD. Lead stays the main thread. |

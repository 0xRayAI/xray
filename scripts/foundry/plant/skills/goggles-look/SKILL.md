# Goggles look

This organ **looks** at one plane's feed. The card is the feed. Quote the plate, the entry, and the exit. Do not sweep another plane.

**Seat:** fastened with `goggles` + `goggles-inspect` when `foundry.json` `"plant": "goggles"` or `"plant": "scout"`. Same plant. Mill may be off. Not a 45-skill dump.

## Spine

1. **Name the plane** on the card.
2. **Open the plate** the card names.
3. **Read the feed** — entry, exit, files on that card.
4. **Stop** at the card's exit. Another plane returns the card.

A feed look is not a sound bed, not a blip replay, and not mill inspect of AI work. Nibbler wears this organ.

npx @0xray/foundry inspect --skip-live
